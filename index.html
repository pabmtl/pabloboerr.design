About me

Currently

OpenAI

I’m a Senior Staff Product Designer at OpenAI, working across Codex, Sonata, agents, and AI product experiences. My work focuses on turning powerful technical systems into product experiences people can understand, guide, and trust.

Before OpenAI, I spent six years at Shopify shaping analytics platforms, data products, mobile experiences, and query tools used at scale. I led work across dashboards, reporting, ShopifyQL, visualization systems, segmentation, and AI-assisted workflows.

My work sits between AI, data, developer tools, and decision-making. Across those spaces, I care about making complex systems feel approachable without flattening their depth.

Good design respects complexity without passing that complexity on to people.
